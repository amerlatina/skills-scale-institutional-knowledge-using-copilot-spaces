# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme manages customer-focused, cross-functional projects through iterative delivery, clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and continuous learning. Its lifecycle moves from initiation and planning through execution and release, then closes with a retrospective and follow-up improvements.

Initiation clarifies the problem, measurable outcomes, stakeholders, timeline, risks, and resources. Planning turns approved work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, milestones, a Definition of Done, and identified dependencies. During execution, teams build, test, review, and iterate in small increments; releases are deployed, verified, and announced, with rollback plans prepared in advance.

The Project Manager coordinates delivery, schedules, risks, dependencies, and communication. The Product Manager owns product outcomes, backlog priorities, and success measures; developers implement and test the work, QA validates quality and acceptance criteria, and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Teams use artifacts such as the project charter or one-pager, roadmap and release plan, iteration backlog, risk register, and retrospective action items to coordinate work.

Communication includes team standups, weekly PM and Product Manager alignment, delivery reviews and demos, and regular stakeholder updates. Risks and blockers are escalated through the team, Project Manager, Product Lead, and sponsor as appropriate. Quality is supported by clear acceptance criteria and Definition of Done, code review, automated tests and linting in CI, security scanning, and release smoke tests and post-deployment verification.
